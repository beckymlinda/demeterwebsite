Our Team

Decades of Malawian agriculture, in one leadership team

Demeter Farm is guided by an experienced leadership team with deep roots in Malawian agriculture — many with decades of combined hands-on farming, agribusiness, and operational experience.

DG

Dimitri Giannakis

Managing Director

Dimitri holds a degree in Agriculture and an M.Sc. in Tropical Agriculture from Wye College, University of London. He co-founded the agro-inputs company Agora Ltd. and went on to become Operations Director of Farmers World Ltd and the Malawi Fertilizer Company. He later co-founded Farmers World Holdings and the Meridian Group, which expanded operations into Zimbabwe, Mozambique, and Zambia, and served as joint CEO of Meridian Consolidated.

Dimitri brings over 20 years of hands-on experience directing agricultural operations, including Demeter Seed Company and Demeter Farm. In 2016, he established the Farm Services Unit, a smallholder farmer extension programme now serving 12,000 farmers.

CG

Christos Giannakis

Managing Director

Christos holds a BSc (Honours) in Economics from Queen Mary College, University of London, and a qualification in Agricultural Development from the University of Reading. He co-founded Agora Ltd. in 1995, which grew into the regional Meridian Group, and served as joint CEO of the Meridian Group and Farmers World Group.

Christos has over 25 years of hands-on experience in arable crop farming and currently serves as co-Director of Demeter Agriculture Ltd. — one of Malawi's largest seed businesses — and co-Director of Demeter Farm.

CS

Chris Stals

General Manager

With 29 years of farming experience across Malawi and Zimbabwe, Chris has managed cattle and tobacco operations and coordinated silo operations for a syndicate of 50 farmers before joining Demeter Farm, where he has now worked for over 20 years.

GM

Geoffrey Munthali

Financial Controller

A qualified ACCA Chartered Accountant, Geoffrey previously worked as a senior auditor at Ernst & Young Malawi and Grant Thornton Malawi. He has been with the Demeter team for over 8 years.

FS

Fil Stals

Administration Manager

Fil oversees farm administration and the Butchery. Prior to joining Demeter Farm, she managed a company supporting farmers' auto-electrical needs in Zimbabwe. She has been with Demeter Farm for over 18 years.

Supporting the leadership team are experienced managers across operations, human resources, and field management — many of whom have been with Demeter Farm for well over a decade, reflecting the strong, stable culture behind our farm.
