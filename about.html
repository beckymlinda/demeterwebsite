About Demeter Farm

40 years of heritage, a 7,600-hectare estate

From our beginnings growing dry beans to becoming an Anchor Farm Hub supporting Malawi's smallholder export sector — this is the story of Demeter Farm, and the estate that makes it possible.

About Us

Two enterprises, one commercial farm

Nestled along the scenic west bank of the Shire River in Balaka, Malawi, Demeter Farm is a 7,600-hectare estate with a legacy dating back over 40 years. As a commercial farm, we specialise in two complementary enterprises: dry bean cultivation and large-scale beef production, supplying both local and international markets.

We are proud to be a pioneer in Malawi's agricultural sector — our estate was duly recognised as an Anchor Farm in a Live Presidential Tour of the Southern Region in 2024 under the Government's Mega Farm Initiative. Our dual-enterprise model allows us to contribute meaningfully to food security, farmer incomes, export growth, and the development of Malawi's agricultural economy.

Our Vision

To be a leading force in the agricultural sector, fostering sustainable growth and contributing significantly to the economic prosperity of Malawi — creating a dynamic and profitable commercial farming business that elevates Malawi's standing in the global market.

Our Mission

To establish a thriving, commercially viable enterprise specialising in irrigated Red Speckled Sugar Bean (RSSB) cultivation and fodder crops, capturing international markets through the export of high-quality produce, while locally supplying improved seed to fellow commercial farmers. We are also proud to operate Malawi's first formal local beef supply chain — rearing quality steers sustainably, processing them in a modern, hygienic abattoir, and distributing value-added meat products to retailers across the country, while supporting local smallholder cattle herders along the way.

Background

Four decades in the making

40+ years growing beans

Demeter Farm has been cultivating beans in Malawi for over 40 years, and specifically developing its expertise in the Shire Valley for the past 20 years.

The "Chuma" variety

Over that time, the farm identified the varieties best suited to both local growing conditions and export markets, eventually developing and registering its own Red Speckled Sugar Bean variety, known as "Chuma."

A Best Practices guide

Through two decades of research, trial, and refinement, Demeter Farm's management team developed a "Best Practices" guide for growing dry beans in the Shire Valley — knowledge that is now shared with satellite and smallholder farmers as part of our extension services.

A complementary farming cycle

In parallel, Demeter Farm built a large-scale cattle ranching operation, integrating it with our bean enterprise: crop residues are used as cattle fodder, and cattle manure is returned to the soil to nourish future bean crops — a sustainable, complementary farming cycle.

Anchor Farm Hub, today

Today, Demeter Farm plays a pioneering role as Anchor Farm Hub. We support a growing cluster of satellite farms by providing certified seed, extension services, and off-take agreements — helping scale up Malawi's dry bean export sector as a whole, while continuing to grow our own beef supply chain to serve both local and regional demand.

Our Estate

7,600 hectares on the Shire River

Demeter Farm spans 7,600 hectares along the scenic west bank of the Shire River in Balaka, Malawi. The estate's strategic location, fertile soils, and access to water make it one of the most productive and versatile agricultural properties in the region — supporting both our crop and livestock operations side by side.
