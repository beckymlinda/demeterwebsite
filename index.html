About Us

Pioneering commercial agriculture in Malawi

Nestled along the scenic west bank of the Shire River in Balaka, Demeter Farm is a 7,600-hectare estate with a legacy dating back over 40 years. As a commercial farm, we specialise in two complementary enterprises — dry bean cultivation and large-scale beef production — supplying both local and international markets.

We are proud to be a pioneer in Malawi's agricultural sector. Our estate was recognised as an Anchor Farm in a Live Presidential Tour of the Southern Region in 2024, under the Government's Mega Farm Initiative.

Our Vision

To be a leading force in the agricultural sector, fostering sustainable growth and contributing significantly to the economic prosperity of Malawi — creating a dynamic and profitable commercial farming business that elevates Malawi's standing in the global market.

Our Mission

To establish a thriving enterprise specialising in irrigated Red Speckled Sugar Bean cultivation, capturing export markets while locally supplying improved seed — and to operate Malawi's first formal local beef supply chain, supporting smallholder herders along the way.

Two Enterprises, One Estate

Beans and beef, grown the sustainable way

Crop residues feed our cattle; cattle manure nourishes our bean fields — a complementary farming cycle built over two decades in the Shire Valley.

Dry Bean Cultivation

Home of "Chuma," our own registered Red Speckled Sugar Bean variety — developed over 20 years of research in the Shire Valley and grown under eight centre-pivot irrigators for export-grade quality.

Visit the Estate

Balaka Beef

A breeding herd of over 1,000 Brahman cross-bred cows and a herd of ~2,500 cattle supply Malawi's first formal local beef supply chain — from pasture, to our own abattoir, to the butchery shelf.
