About Balaka Beef

One of Malawi's first fully integrated beef supply chains

Balaka Beef is Demeter Farm's beef production and processing enterprise — one of Malawi's first fully integrated, formal beef supply chains, from pasture to plate.

Our operation includes a breeding herd of over 1,000 Brahman cross-bred cows, supporting a total herd of around 2,500 cattle, alongside a feedlot that rears approximately 1,500 young cattle every year. Cattle are sourced both from our own breeding herd and from local smallholder cattle herders in the surrounding districts, giving those communities reliable, regular off-take for their animals.

Cattle are humanely processed at our modern, on-site abattoir and prepared for market in our own butchery, before being distributed to wholesale and retail outlets, restaurants, and hotels across the Southern and Central Regions of Malawi.

Our Role

Building Malawi's beef industry

  • Supporting local smallholder herders with regular weekly off-take, improving their incomes and livelihoods.
  • Providing extension services and animal husbandry support to improve herd quality.
  • Delivering reliable cold-chain logistics from farm to retailer.
  • Championing import substitution — reducing Malawi's reliance on imported beef.
  • Upholding high standards of hygiene, quality, and disease control in every stage of processing.
  • Practicing climate-smart, sustainable grazing and herd management.

Balaka Beef is proud to be building the foundation of a fully formalised national beef supply chain — supporting local herders, meeting rising demand, and delivering quality, trustworthy beef to communities across Malawi.
